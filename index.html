About Us

Guiding Ambitions Beyond Borders with Care, Clarity, and Commitment

Nayak Consultancy Services is a premier recruitment agency based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka. Under the visionary leadership of our founder, **Saibanna Nayak**, we have grown to become a trusted pathway for overseas employment opportunities. **Mr. Saibanna Nayak** started this agency with a simple, core mission: to provide genuine, ethical, and legal global career choices for local talents, protecting them from unauthorized mediators.

Today, we specialize in providing tailored placements for permanent contracts, trade test evaluations, and complete embassy visa operations to ensure candidates land smoothly in their target destinations.

International Hiring Process

1. Apply Online

3. Employer Interview

4. Documentation
